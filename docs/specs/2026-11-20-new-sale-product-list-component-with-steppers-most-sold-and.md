## Goal
This task delivers New sale's Product list as a component that no screen mounts yet. It is for a seller putting a sale together on the phone. The seller finds each Product the customer asked for and sets how much of it with a tap or two. Every figure comes from that day's prices through the existing pricing functions. The list shows what is already in the sale and what usually sells. It shows a plain hint when nothing can be sold, and no figure before its data arrives. It also delivers the `useNewSaleReads` hook, the copy for the list, and component tests. Until the flip part of GH-496 mounts it, only the tests reach it.

## Acceptance criteria
- `useNewSaleReads({ date, openedOn })`:
  - reads Products (`GET /api/products?on=<date>`, key `saleFormVarieties(date)`) and pricing settings (key `saleFormPricingSettings(date)`) with exactly the options `NuevaVenta.tsx` uses;
  - gets suggestions only through `useSaleSuggestions(openedOn)`;
  - returns `{ products, productsForDate, settings, mostSoldIds, failed }` as the request defines them.
- A row shows the Product name, "{stock} in stock" (0 and negative figures as they are), the price or "No price", and the steps.
- A row not in the sale is priced only by `rowPrices`. A line shows its `pricePerGram` from `priceSale` called only with `saleLineInputsOf(draft.state.lines)` and shipping 0. Both are memoized, and neither runs while `customerTypeId` is 'pending'.
- A row not in the sale that has a price shows "+" named "Add {name}", which calls `addProduct`. A Product with zero or negative Stock can still be added.
- A line shows, inside a group named by the Product, "−" ("Remove one {name}", `stepDown`), the read-only quantity (`formatQuantity` of `quantityHundredths`) and "+" ("Add one {name}", `stepUp`). A line with no price shows "No price", "−" and the quantity, with no "+".
- The component does no arithmetic on money or quantities. "−" from 1 removes the line, and 1.5 − 1 leaves 0.5, both through the draft. There is at most one line per Product.
- Buttons are at least 44x44 px, with `user-select: none` and `touch-action: manipulation`. The glyphs are hidden from accessibility.
- With the search empty:
  - "In the sale · {n}" lists the lines in the order they were added, only when there are lines;
  - "Most sold" follows, only when it has rows. It keeps the suggestions' order, drops Products already in the sale and ids the Products read did not return, and applies the `MOST_SOLD_ROWS` cap after that filtering.
- With text in the search:
  - "Results · {n}" lists the matches in name order, ignoring case and accents;
  - the match is highlighted with a mark element at weight 600 and the accent token;
  - with no match, only "Results · 0" shows.
- The search field:
  - has the visually hidden label "Search products" and the placeholder "Search by name";
  - is `type=search`, `inputMode=search`, `autoComplete=off`, `enterKeyHint=search`;
  - has a font size of at least 16 px.
- The hint:
  - shows `selectorNoVarieties` or `NO_PRICE_HINT`, from `nothingSellable(products)`, with the Attention icon, under the search;
  - shows only when `productsForDate` is true, the search is empty and nothing is pending;
  - follows the sale's date.
- Before the first full load, `MOST_SOLD_ROWS` skeleton rows show with `aria-busy` true (via `LoadingWhile`), with no header, digit, currency symbol or hint.
- On a later date or Customer change:
  - the names and the steppers of lines stay;
  - the date-dependent cells show the compact loading mark;
  - the list is `aria-busy` and the hint is hidden;
  - nothing re-prices or flashes while the Customer is 'pending'.
- A `NetworkError` on Products or pricing settings shows `SinConexion`, and any other error shows `ErrorGenerico`. Either replaces the list, with no `aria-busy`. A failed suggestions read shows no error and no "Most sold".
- A change of the sale's date makes 0 new suggestions requests.
- The ten `newSale` copy keys exist in `web/src/copy/sales.ts` with the agreed English texts. They are registered in LIMITED_COPY and BASELINE_ARGS, and `en.baseline.json` is regenerated by the baseline step.
- No route, navigation entry or screen mounts `NuevaVentaProductos`. No route, flag, query parameter, `data-*` hook, prop or export exists only for tests. The harness lives inside the test file.
- Color, type and spacing come only from design tokens.
- `NuevaVentaProductos.test.tsx` (Testing Library at fetch) covers every case listed in the request.
- vitest, tsc strict and oxlint pass, with nothing silenced.
- The PR attaches the Design capture against NuevaVentaProductos and NuevaVentaProductosOscuro, light and dark. It states that content shift and blank-while-loading are judged on the flip part's walk.

## Out of scope
- Any route, navigation entry or screen that mounts the list (the flip part of GH-496).
- The Customer, payment and saving steps of New sale.
- Barcode and category (accepted departure 2).
- Any server change.

## Decisions
- The list sets no step and does no arithmetic on quantities: "+" and "−" move one whole unit, as the sale in progress decides.
  - Reason: one quantity rule, in the draft, so the screen never changes what the sale holds on its own.
- A Product with zero or negative Stock can still be added; only a missing price blocks adding.
  - Reason: Stock only informs and never blocks a Sale (glossary, Stock).
- "In the sale" lists the lines in the order they were added; "Most sold" keeps the suggestions' order.
  - Reason: the section reads like the customer's order, and a line never jumps while it is stepped.
- The price shows no unit label.
  - Reason: Assume nothing (criterion 4); Units are still planned in GH-318.
- Any difference from the drawings other than departure 2 is raised to the owner on the PR, never accepted as an assumption.
  - Reason: only the owner decides a departure from the drawings.
- The unmounted module may be added to `web/scripts/docs/front-exclude.json`, and the flip part removes the entry.
  - Reason: the owner's rule D-62e6dc23.

## Risks
The drawings may show a unit suffix or another element that this build leaves out. Such a difference needs the owner's decision on the PR.
Several reads must all arrive before anything shows, and some states are in between (date or Customer pending). A bug there could flash a wrong price for a moment, so the tests must cover each pending state.
