## Goal

Add a "Date, shipping and note" row to the not-yet-routed Collect Sheet, along with a re-pricing note, a loading mark and a no-price warning. A seller who is about to collect and realises the sale happened on another day, has shipping to charge or needs a note can fix it without going back to New sale. They see the total at that date's prices and never save at a total they did not see. The usual sale (today, no shipping, no note) costs nothing extra. Front only (GH-496 part 9d, part 4 of 4 of GH-868). Nothing mounts the Sheet until the flip part of GH-496.

## Acceptance criteria

- New `web/src/screens/ventas/DateShippingNoteRow.tsx` renders one collapsed row titled "Date, shipping and note" (never "More"), with `aria-expanded`. It is listed in `web/scripts/docs/front-exclude.json`.
- The row sits directly above the Total row, inside the `cobro__bloque` block with `inert={saving}`. Total, How they pay, Received and Change, and Collect keep their order.
- The collapsed summary joins with " · ", in this order, only the values that are not at their default:
  - the date as dd/mm when it is not the Business's today;
  - "Shipping {amount}" when the shipping is above 0;
  - "Note" when the Note is not blank, never its text.
- With every value at its default, the collapsed row shows only its title.
- The expanded row shows, in order:
  - a date control (type date, required, autocomplete off, max the Business's today) that writes `draft.setDate`, where a refused value goes back to the last valid one;
  - the re-pricing note, when there is one;
  - Shipping charged through `MoneyInput`, with its existing label and refusal;
  - Note, with autocomplete off.
- Each input of the row carries the checked phone attributes, asserted in the row's own test.
- `CollectSheet.tsx` takes `pricing: NewSalePricing` instead of `priced`. It renders only from `collectViewOf`, and it snapshots `{date, total}` in a `useState` initializer each time it opens.
- A date change refetches `/api/products?on=<date>` and re-prices the lines in place, without reordering them.
- The re-pricing note:
  - reads "Prices updated for {dd/mm}", with no Attention icon;
  - shows under the date in both the collapsed and the expanded state;
  - appears only after a change of both date and total;
  - never appears after a shipping-only change;
  - goes away when the date goes back;
  - is gone after Close and reopen.
- While the new date's prices load or fail:
  - the Total, the Collect label and the note show the compact loading mark;
  - no figure is shown and Change shows nothing;
  - a tap on Collect sends 0 requests.
- Each line with no price on the new date shows one warning, "{name} has no price on {dd/mm}" (`noPriceOnDate`), with the Attention icon. While any warning stands:
  - the Total shows no figure;
  - there is no loading mark;
  - the Collect label has no amount;
  - Collect sends 0 requests.
- The no-price warning goes away when the date goes back.
- Clearing or refusing the date keeps the last valid date, its request and its total.
- `collect()` sends nothing unless `canSend`. Only `collectStateOf`, read through `collectViewOf`, decides whether Collect can send.
- During a held save the row is inert, Close does nothing, and the `POST /api/sales` body carries the date that was showing at the tap.
- The CollectSheet header sentence about a silent no-op is rewritten.
- Every new text lives in `web/src/copy/sales.ts`, with sample arguments in `baselineArgs.ts`. Each is in English with no Unit or product-type word. `en.baseline.json` is regenerated by its script, never merged by hand.
- The existing CollectSheet tests are moved to a harness on `useNewSalePricing` and still pass.
- `priceSale` appears outside tests only in `sales/pricing.ts` and `sales/newSalePricing.ts`.
- No route, build constant, query parameter, window flag or `data-*` hook exists only for tests.
- The Collect sections of `docs/product/sale-in-progress.md` are updated. "A Collect Sheet text for a line with no price" moves from out of scope into the Collect section, with a date change as its cause.
- CI jobs web, vitest, docs and ci-ok pass.

## Out of scope

- Any route or navigation entry that mounts the Collect Sheet, and the flip rule that keeps an unpriced line from opening it.
- Adding or changing a price from the Collect Sheet.
- Rejection texts for the date, and a lower bound for the sale date.
- A failure text or a retry for a failed price read of the new date.
- Making the date more prominent; that is a later polish task.
- Saving anything beyond the date, Shipping charged and Note already on the Sale in progress.
- Any server change.

## Decisions

- The row sits directly above the Total row, collapsed by default. Expanded, it shows the date, then the re-pricing note, then Shipping charged, then Note.
  - Reason: The effect of the date on the total has to be read next to the Total. A sale with every value at its default looks as it did before, plus one quiet title row.
- The re-pricing note reads "Prices updated for {dd/mm}", carries no Attention icon, and shows in both the collapsed and the expanded state.
  - Reason: It is a plain notice that asks for no action. The seller has to see that the sale was re-priced even without opening the row.
- While any no-price warning stands, the Total shows no figure, there is no loading mark, and the Collect label has no amount.
  - Reason: A total that leaves a line out would break the promise that the seller never saves at a total they did not see. Nothing is loading, so a loading mark would say the wrong thing.
- The no-price warning names the Product and the date and does not send the seller away to add a price.
  - Reason: Adding a price is a separate flow. The seller resolves it on this Sheet by moving the date back, or with a Gift or Special price.
- With no total (prices loading or failed, or an unpriced line), Change shows nothing.
  - Reason: Change must never be computed from a total the seller cannot see.
- The row, note, loading mark and warning are new behaviour with no drawing of their own. They are compared by eye with NuevaVentaCobrar and its dark twin, and their Design capture stays "not compared" until the flip.
  - Reason: No approved drawing shows them, and the Sheet has no route until the flip. Any visible difference the owner would read differently goes to the owner.

## Risks

- A failed price read of the new date shows only the loading mark, with no reason given. The seller can still move the date back or Close; the flip decides whether a failure text is needed.
- The Sheet is not routed, so the row and its states are not walked on a real phone or compared side by side until the flip.
