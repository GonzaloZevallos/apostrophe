## Goal

This part gives the seller building a sale on the phone a line detail. Tapping a line in New sale opens it, and there they change its quantity, make it a Gift, give it a Special price, or remove it. They never leave New sale and never lose the customer or the other lines. A line with no price shows "No price" with the Attention icon instead of $0, and tapping it opens the same detail. This is GH-496 part 9c, part 3 of 4 of GH-868. It is front only and not routed, and it serves the Brief's first job: a sale recorded from the phone in under a minute.

## Acceptance criteria

- `web/src/screens/ventas/LineDetailSheet.tsx` renders inside the shared Sheet with these parts:
  - the Product name as title
  - Close on top
  - Done (`lineDetailDone`) fixed at the bottom
  - in the body, in this order: the quantity field (`inputmode` decimal, `autocomplete` off), the Gift control, the Special price through `MoneyInput` with its existing help line, and "Remove line" (`removeLineButton`) last
- The Sheet keeps a local buffer started from the line and validates it only through `lineEditOf`.
- Done is unavailable while `lineEditOf` refuses. The reason shows under the field: the existing quantity text for empty, 0, three-decimal and 14-digit quantities, and `specialPriceAboveZero` for a Special price of 0.
- Done dispatches `editLine` exactly once, closes, and the row total updates. With an unchanged buffer, Done closes and dispatches nothing.
- Close and the back gesture discard the buffer with no confirmation. The line stays as it was.
- Gift and Special price exclude each other: turning Gift on clears and disables the Special price, and typing a Special price turns Gift off.
- Remove line acts at once with no confirmation, notice or Undo, closes, and returns to the draft. On the last line the draft is clean, the sale bar has no Collect, and the customer, date, shipping and Note are unchanged.
- In `NuevaVentaProductos`, the name and price area of a line is one control with the accessible name `lineOpenDetail(name)`, and it opens the detail. "−", the quantity and "+" never open it.
- A line whose `PricedSaleLine.pricing` is null shows `lineNoPrice` with the shared Attention icon in the price slot, never $0, with "−" and the quantity and no "+". Tapping it opens the detail.
- `NuevaVentaSheet` holds which line is open. Closing the detail never triggers "Discard the sale?".
- New copy keys are in `web/src/copy/sales.ts`, registered in LIMITED_COPY and BASELINE_ARGS. `en.baseline.json` is regenerated by its script.
- `LineDetailSheet.tsx` is listed in `web/scripts/docs/front-exclude.json`.
- Every text input in the line detail and the row carries the checked phone attributes, asserted in the component's own test.
- Testing Library tests at fetch level cover: the exclusion, a Special price of 0, the bad quantities, Done updating the row total, Close discarding, Remove line including the last line, the "No price" row opening the detail, and the phone attributes.
- Only the line detail paragraphs of `docs/product/sale-in-progress.md` change.
- CI jobs web, vitest, docs and ci-ok pass.

## Out of scope

- Collect and the other stacked Sheets.
- The date control and the date refusal texts.
- The routing flip of GH-496, WalkState and `screens.json` entries, Design capture and Screen image.
- Any change to how lines are priced.
- A notice or Undo for Remove line, a confirmation on Close or on Remove line, a chevron or "Edit" mark on the line, and focus behaviour of its own.
- Typing a quantity in the list itself.
- Any route, build constant, query parameter, window flag or `data-*` hook made for tests.
- Any server change.

## Decisions

- Done is available when the buffer equals the line, and it then closes without dispatching `editLine`.
  - Reason: a seller who only opened a line to look at it must never get stuck. Only a refusal blocks Done.
- The `giftWithSpecial` refusal gets no text of its own.
  - Reason: the Sheet's Gift and Special price controls exclude each other, so the UI never produces it. If it ever arrives, Done stays unavailable like for any refusal.
- A line whose Product is "no longer available" also opens the detail, titled and named with the shared "no longer available" text.
  - Reason: its pricing is null, so the same row rule applies. The existing doc already names its group and "−" with that text and forbids ids or guessed names.
- Lines in the sale use `lineNoPrice` with the Attention icon. Rows not in the sale keep `productNoPrice` with no icon.
  - Reason: the request scopes the icon to lines, which the seller must fix. A row outside the sale only can't be added.
- The "No price" line is not a Tappable notice.
  - Reason: its accessible name is `lineOpenDetail(name)`, not the notice sentence, so the glossary term doesn't apply.
- The line detail is mounted by `NuevaVentaSheet`, not by the New sale Host.
  - Reason: the request puts the open line in `NuevaVentaSheet`, and closing a stacked Sheet goes back one level without the leave guard.

## Risks

- The back gesture on the stacked line detail could reach the Host's leave guard and ask "Discard the sale?" by mistake. Tests must close it one level only.
- Done's "unavailable" look comes from the shared Sheet. It may read poorly on the phone, and it can't be judged before the flip because the screen has no route.
- The look of the detail and the "No price" row is "not compared" until the flip mounts the screen.
